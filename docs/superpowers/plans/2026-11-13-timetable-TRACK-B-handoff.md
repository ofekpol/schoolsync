  weekly timetable itself: the principal enters teachers, classes and lessons, teachers enter

  staff pick theirs; the principal filters staff by label. Labels never grant access.

## Words we agreed on

- **Grade** = ז, ח, ט ... (stored as 7 to 12). **Class** = ז1, ז2, ז3 ...
- **Plata** = several classes of one grade having the same lesson at the same period. A class
  is "in the Plata" or "out of the Plata".
- **Lesson block** = the unit the solver places: one subject, weekly periods, a set of classes
  and a set of teachers who are all busy together. An ordinary lesson has one class; a Plata
  has several. There is no separate Plata table.
- **Board** = one timetable project of a school for a school year (`tt_boards`). Classes,
  subjects and teachers belong to a board.
- **Meeting** = a constraint type: a label or a list of teachers shares N weekly periods. The
  export turns it into a block with no classes.
- Product name and URL: **timeTable**, `/timetable`. (`/schedule` is the Gantt's guest view.)

## Decisions already made (do not reopen without talking to Ofek)

- School `admin` = the principal. Teachers are regular staff users, one school per account.
- No rooms, no parents, no email, no imports, not in the public `/demo` sandbox, grades ז to יב.
- A board's week starts from one default in code (Sunday to Thursday 8 periods, Friday 5).
  Per-school weeks on `/platform/schools` are deferred.
- Teachers enter only binding constraints (free day, if entitled). Management enters the rest,
  aimed at a teacher, a class, a grade, a subject or a profile label.
- Constraint types and what the solver receives for each are frozen in the table under plan 6
  in the split document. You build the form and Zod schema of a type
  (`lib/timetable/constraints/<type>.ts`); Ofek builds its encoder in the solver.
- The solver never receives names or labels, only ids. A label is turned into teacher ids by
  the export.
- The solver always returns a result and lists what it had to break. Three options = three
  priority settings (teachers first, classes first, balanced).
- Solver: Python with Google OR-Tools (CP-SAT). Where it runs is decided by measurement in
  plan 4. Constraint rules exist only in the solver.
- Profile categories are free labels. They are not linked to the timetable's classes and
  subjects; the demo seed reads the category named "מקצוע" to decide who teaches what.

## Staging and the demo data

- Staging is a copy of production, so `kfar-galim` on staging holds real staff names. Do not
  paste them into a chat or a PR. Screenshots for other schools come from a board seeded with

  `/platform/schools`, re-enter the labels, run `pnpm seed:timetable-demo`.
